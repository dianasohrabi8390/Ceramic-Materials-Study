# Ceramic Materials Study: BaTiO₃, HfC, and ZnO

## 1. Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a university ceramics course at the Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Sharif University of Technology.

The project provides a technical study of three ceramic materials: **Barium Titanate (BaTiO₃), Hafnium Carbide (HfC), and Zinc Oxide (ZnO)**. It explores their crystal structures, atomic arrangements, physical properties, synthesis methods, and engineering applications.

## 2. Materials Studied

### Barium Titanate (BaTiO₃)

A functional ceramic material known for its ferroelectric and dielectric properties.

Topics covered:
- Crystal structure and atomic arrangement
- Pauling's rules and coordination numbers
- Theoretical density calculation
- Phase diagrams and synthesis methods
- Industrial and electronic applications

### Hafnium Carbide (HfC)

An ultra-high-temperature ceramic known for its exceptionally high melting temperature, hardness, and potential for extreme-environment applications.

Topics covered:
- Crystal structure and lattice parameters
- Theoretical density calculation
- Phase diagrams and related carbide systems
- Synthesis methods
- Aerospace and high-temperature applications

### Zinc Oxide (ZnO)

A functional ceramic semiconductor with important optical, electrical, and piezoelectric properties.

Topics covered:
- Crystal structures and atomic arrangements
- Wurtzite structure and coordination
- Physical and functional properties
- Engineering and technological applications

## 3. Technical Concepts
